He always goes first!

A companion, not a digital game. The market, the token supply and your cards stay on the table. The app replaces the automa deck, the token board and every bit of Thanos’s bookkeeping.

How a round goes

Four taps a round

  1. 1

    Flip his card

    Thanos always goes first. The app shuffles his deck for each phase and reads the right section of the card.

  2. 2

    Do what he says

    Which tokens he takes, which card he recruits, the colour substitution, and which tokens go back first: you just move them on the table.

  3. 3

    Play your turn

    Tick off the table checklist, play as usual, and log the card you recruited: its level, colour, points and Avengers tags.

  4. 4

    Race to the Gauntlet

    At the end of every round the app checks both sides for the Infinity Gauntlet, with the official tiebreakers. Survive 30 rounds or it’s a SNAP.

What it does

Thanos, fully automated

  • Runs his deck

    Shuffles each phase, reads green, yellow, then red, and keeps the 30-round clock.

  • Tells you exactly what he does

    Tokens in printed order, the slot he recruits, colour substitution and the oldest tokens back first.

  • Keeps his bookkeeping

    His token piles, cards, points, the Avengers tile and the Time stone.

  • Checks the Gauntlet

    Every round, for both sides, with the official tiebreakers and the automa’s rules for ties.

  • Undo anything

    Every step can be undone, and the game is saved after every tap.

  • Made for the table

    Installs to your home screen, works offline and keeps the screen awake during a game.

  • i

    Rules where you need them

    Tap the “i” next to a step to read the rule it follows, right at the table.

  • ?

    Rulings for the gaps

    Where the automa PDF is silent, the defaults follow the BoardGameGeek clarifications, and you can change them.

Five languages

Speaks your language

It starts in your phone’s language, and you can switch any time. Every text, including the rules and Thanos’s lines, is translated.

Run it yourself

Host your own copy

It’s a static web app with no server code, accounts or tracking. Build the Docker image, or the static files, and serve them over HTTPS so phones can install it.
